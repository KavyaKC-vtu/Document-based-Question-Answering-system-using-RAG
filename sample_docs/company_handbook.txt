Acme Corp Employee Handbook - 2024

Welcome to Acme Corp!

Section 1: Leave Policy
- Annual Leave: 24 days per year, accrued monthly. Unused leave can be carried over up to 5 days.
- Sick Leave: 12 days per year, requires medical certificate if >2 days consecutive.
- Remote Work: Up to 3 days per week remote after probation (3 months). Core hours 10am-3pm must be online.

Section 2: Benefits
- Health Insurance: Comprehensive plan covering employee + spouse + 2 children. Dental up to $1000/year.
- Learning Budget: $1500 per year for courses, books, and conferences.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid for primary caregiver, 4 weeks for secondary.

Section 3: IT Security
- Passwords must be 12+ characters and changed every 90 days.
- MFA is mandatory for all internal tools.
- Do not share customer data via personal email or unencrypted channels. Report phishing to security@acme.corp.

Section 4: Expense Reimbursement
- Submit via Expensify within 30 days.
- Manager approval required for >$500.
- Travel: Economy flights for <6 hours, Business for longer.

Section 5: Code of Conduct
- Treat everyone with respect. Zero tolerance for harassment.
- Office hours: 9am-6pm, flexible with manager approval.

For questions, contact hr@acme.corp or visit the intranet.
